Spells CARL

How to play

  1. Pick any spell from the list.
  2. Add one letter, change one letter, or remove one letter. Only one.
  3. Describe what the new spell does. Loudest laugh wins.

CARL is the game itself: Change, Add, or Remove a Letters.
